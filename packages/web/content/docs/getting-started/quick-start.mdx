There are two ways in. The live demo needs nothing from you. Your own run needs a workload and a SERV key.

## 1. Try the live demo

The demo runs one of our sample agents, a payables agent that decides whether to pay, hold or reject a supplier invoice. It is paid from our own SERV key under a daily budget, so you need no key and pay nothing.

1. Open [/try](/try).
2. Under "Pick a sample agent", choose one of the three:
   - "Data in the wrong place": the supplier book pasted into the system prompt, the mistake the setup check catches.
   - "The same agent, set up right": rules in the system prompt, supplier data in the user message.
   - "The 152-rule hard rulebook": 152 clauses from four layered rule sources.
   Each row also shows the result of that sample's saved full run, with a link to "Open the saved report".
3. Press "Run it live".
4. Urai runs the first 12 cases of the sample, once with SERV off and once with SERV on, three calls at a time. Each call takes about 5 to 15 seconds, because the model really reads the whole rulebook. You watch every answer land in the grid, marked right or wrong against the expected verdict.
5. When "Every answer is back.", Urai publishes the report and gives you "Open the full report".

If the day's demo budget runs out mid-run, the page says "The demo budget is used up for now.", keeps the answers that already came back, and points you to the saved full reports instead.

> Note: the demo only runs our samples, with the settings we allow for them. Your own text never travels on our key. That rule is C4 in the [threat model](/docs/security/threat-model).

## 2. Run your own agent

You need three things: your agent's system prompt, the JSON Schema of its answer, and a set of cases where you know the right answer. The format is on [Workload format](/docs/getting-started/workload-format). You also need a SERV key: see [Your SERV key](/docs/getting-started/serv-key).

1. Open [/new](/new). Nothing is saved or spent until you press Start.
2. "Start from a sample" (optional): each button fills every field with one of our sample agents, so you can see a finished setup first. Running a sample on your key spends your credit like any other run.
3. "Your agent": fill in Name and System prompt. Put tables, lists and records every case needs in Shared data, not in the system prompt.
4. "Answer shape": paste your Answer JSON Schema, then press "Add a scoring rule" for each field that decides whether an answer is right. Pick Exact match, "Number, with tolerance" or "One of a list".
5. "Test cases": paste a JSON array or a CSV into "Cases, JSON or CSV", or press "Upload CSV".
6. "Setup check": it runs by itself a couple of seconds after you stop typing, or press "Check setup". If it offers "Fix layout", press it. You can press "Undo the fix".
7. "Settings to compare": pick the Model, then tick the settings. SERV off and SERV plain side by side is the fair first test.
8. "Your SERV key": paste it into "SERV API key".
9. "Run it": check the calls count and the estimate, then press "Start the run".

Urai saves your test set, opens the run at `/run/<id>`, and starts sending cases. The full walk-through, including pause, resume and what happens if SERV refuses your key, is on [Run your agent](/docs/guides/run-your-agent).
